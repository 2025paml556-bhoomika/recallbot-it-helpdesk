# Joiner Onboarding

## Before day one

HR creates the employee record at least five working days before the start date. That record drives the account, the mailbox and the laptop build. A start date inside those five days is handled as an exception and the laptop may arrive after day one.

## What is ready on day one

The joiner receives an account, a mailbox with the 50 GB quota, a laptop from the current refresh standard, and membership of the VPN remote-access group. The authenticator app is enrolled during the first-morning session with the buddy, at which point the ten fallback codes are issued and the joiner stores them in SW-PWM-01.

Access to team-specific systems is requested by the manager, not by the joiner. The default set is email, intranet, chat and the software portal.

## The first week

The joiner completes the acceptable-use acknowledgement and the security module before the end of day three. Until the acknowledgement is recorded, access to shared drives stays read-only. The manager confirms the role's software from the managed list in the same week.

## Contractors

Contractors follow the same flow, with two differences. The sponsoring manager sets an end date, and the account is created to expire on that date. VPN access ends with the account.
