# Email and Mailboxes

## Quotas

A user mailbox holds 50 GB. A shared mailbox holds 100 GB. At 90 percent full the owner gets a warning; at 100 percent the mailbox stops accepting mail. Archive mail older than one year to the online archive, which does not count against the quota.

## Shared mailboxes

A shared mailbox has its own credential, held by the mailbox owner. Members are granted access; they do not learn the password. Send-as is turned on only when the owner asks, and it is reviewed every six months.

## Forwarding and auto-replies

Forwarding to a personal address is blocked by rule. Forwarding to another company mailbox is allowed and must be visible to the sender, so silent redirects are rejected. Out-of-office replies should name the person covering and should not include a personal phone number.

## Retention

Mail is kept for seven years, including deleted items, because of the finance retention rule. A user cannot shorten this. A legal hold stops deletion entirely until the legal team releases it, and a hold is recorded against the mailbox rather than against one folder.

## Phishing

Report a suspicious message with the Report Phishing button, which is deployed to every Outlook profile. Do not forward the message to colleagues. The mail gateway quarantines the sender for the whole company once three people report the same message.
