# Laptop Refresh Programme (2025)

This version was retired on 31 December 2025. Orders placed in 2025 were built from the catalogue below. For any refresh requested in 2026, use the 2026 programme.

## Standard builds in 2025

The default Windows laptop was the LAP-T14G3, a 14 inch ThinkPad with 16 GB of memory and a 512 GB drive. The default macOS laptop was the LAP-MBP14, a 14 inch MacBook Pro with 16 GB of memory and a 512 GB drive. Both were on a four-year refresh.

Staff could not choose a higher specification without a director's approval. Docks and monitors were a separate request and did not reset the four-year clock.

## How a refresh was raised

The service desk raised the request when the asset record showed the device was within 90 days of its fourth anniversary, or when a hardware fault was uneconomical to repair. The user confirmed the delivery address and the data-migration window. Old devices were returned within fourteen days of the new one arriving.

## What changed

The 2026 programme shortens the cycle to three years and replaces the LAP-T14G3 with the LAP-T14G5 as the standard Windows build. The LAP-MBP14 remains the standard macOS build.
