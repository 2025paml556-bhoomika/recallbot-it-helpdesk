# Printing and the Follow-me Queue

## The queue

Every office prints through one follow-me queue, named PRINT-FOLLOWME. The user sends the job from the laptop and releases it at any device on that floor by tapping their badge. Jobs that nobody releases are deleted after four hours and are not recoverable.

Colour printing is allowed. Jobs over fifty pages ask for confirmation on the device panel, to catch accidents.

## Secure documents

A job marked confidential is held in a separate lane and requires the badge plus the network PIN. It is deleted after one hour rather than four. Do not print credentials, and do not print customer data that the ticket can link to instead.

## Faults

A jam, a streak, or an offline device is reported on the small-form code displayed on the printer itself. Quote the printer asset tag, which starts with the site code. Do not switch the printer off to clear a jam; open the panel shown on the screen.

## Home and VPN

There is no supported way to print to a home printer while the VPN is connected, because split tunnelling is off. Print before connecting, or print to PRINT-FOLLOWME and collect it at the office.
