# Hardware Catalogue

## Laptops

LAP-T14G5 is the standard Windows laptop for 2026: 14 inch, 32 GB of memory, 1 TB drive, three-year cycle. It replaces the LAP-T14G3, which is no longer ordered.

LAP-MBP14 is the standard macOS laptop: 14 inch, 32 GB of memory, 1 TB drive, three-year cycle.

LAP-P16 is the mobile workstation for approved roles only: 16 inch, 64 GB of memory, 1 TB drive. It needs a manager's approval and a one-line justification, as the refresh programme describes.

## Docks and displays

DOCK-USB4 is the standard dock, with two display outputs and a wired network port. One dock is issued per desk-based user. MON-27Q is the standard 27 inch display, and a desk may have one or two. Both are requested in the same portal as a laptop and do not reset the laptop's refresh clock.

## What cannot be ordered

Personal-preference keyboards, trackballs and headsets are not in the catalogue. Teams that need a headset for a call-centre role order HDST-CC1, which is managed and returned with the laptop.

## Asset tags

Every device carries an asset tag recorded against its holder. Quote the tag in any hardware ticket. A device with no tag is reported to the asset team before it is used.
