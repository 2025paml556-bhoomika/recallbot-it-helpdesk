# Acceptable Use

## Company devices

A company laptop is for company work. Light personal use is tolerated where it does not affect work, breach another policy, or store personal data that the user would not want backed up. The backup service copies the profile folders, so personal tax files and personal photos kept there will be copied onto company storage.

## Data

Company data stays in company systems: the mailbox, the shared drives and the approved software. It is not copied to personal email, personal cloud storage or removable media. A genuine need to move data out, for example a regulator requesting a file, goes through the data-handling request and is approved by the data owner.

## Accounts

Each person uses their own account. Sharing a password, letting a colleague work under your sign-in, or using a shared mailbox credential for ordinary mail breaches this policy. The password policy describes the approved store, SW-PWM-01.

## Monitoring

Traffic on the VPN and on CORP-WIFI is logged. The logs are used for fault finding and for security investigations. They are not used for measuring working hours.

## Consequences

A first minor breach is a conversation with the manager and a repeated acknowledgement. A breach that exposes customer or employee data is an incident and follows the severity note.
