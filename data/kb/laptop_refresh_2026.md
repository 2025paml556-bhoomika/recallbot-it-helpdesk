# Laptop Refresh Programme (2026)

This programme replaces the 2025 version for every refresh requested on or after 6 January 2026.

## Standard builds

The standard Windows laptop is the LAP-T14G5, a 14 inch ThinkPad with 32 GB of memory and a 1 TB drive. The standard macOS laptop remains the LAP-MBP14, now supplied with 32 GB of memory and a 1 TB drive. The refresh cycle is three years from the date the asset was issued.

The older LAP-T14G3 is no longer ordered. Devices of that model already in use stay with their user until the three-year date, and are then swapped for a LAP-T14G5. A machine that fails before its date is repaired if the repair costs less than half the residual value; otherwise it is replaced early and the cycle restarts.

## Raising a refresh

The asset system opens a draft request 90 days before the three-year date and emails the user. The user confirms three things: the delivery site, the migration window, and whether a dock is still held. Confirming late does not extend the old machine's support, which ends on the anniversary.

Delivery to an office takes five working days. Delivery to a home address in India takes eight working days. The old laptop goes back in the same packaging within ten working days. Data on the old drive is wiped against the certificate standard on the day it reaches the build room.

## Exceptions

A role that needs a mobile workstation, such as video editing or firmware builds, can request the LAP-P16 instead of the standard build. The request needs the manager's approval and a one-line justification. Memory above the standard build is not offered, because the standard build already has 32 GB.
