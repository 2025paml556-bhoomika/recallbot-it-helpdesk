# Password and MFA Policy (2025)

This version was retired on 31 December 2025. It is kept so that old tickets can be read in context. For every new request, follow the 2026 policy.

## Password rules in force during 2025

A network password had to be at least 12 characters long and contain three of the four kinds of character: lowercase, uppercase, digits and symbols. Users changed it every 90 days. The previous five passwords could not be reused.

Passwords were typed into the Windows and macOS sign-in screen and into Cisco AnyConnect. Writing a password on paper was not allowed. Sharing a password, even with a manager or the service desk, was a conduct matter.

## Multi-factor authentication in 2025

MFA was required for VPN, webmail and the self-service portal. The approved factor was a six-digit code from an authenticator app, entered by hand. SMS codes were not accepted. A lost phone was handled by the service desk, which issued a single-use backup code after a video call to confirm identity.

## What changed

The 2026 policy removed the 90-day expiry, lengthened the minimum, and replaced typed codes with push approval. Do not apply the 90-day rule to any account after 31 December 2025.
