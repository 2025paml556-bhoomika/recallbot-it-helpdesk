# Office Wi-Fi

## Networks

Offices broadcast two networks. CORP-WIFI is for company laptops and uses certificate authentication, so there is no password to type or share. CORP-GUEST is for visitors and personal phones. A guest pass lasts eight hours, is created by any employee from the intranet, and throttles each device to 5 megabits per second.

## Connecting a company laptop

Company laptops are preconfigured for CORP-WIFI and connect on their own inside any office. If a laptop keeps associating and then dropping, forget the network and restart. Do not create a manual profile and do not type a password, because CORP-WIFI has none.

## What is blocked

CORP-WIFI blocks peer-to-peer file sharing and wireless displays that are not on the approved list. CORP-GUEST blocks access to every internal system, including the intranet and shared drives. A visitor who needs to show an internal document uses a company laptop, not the guest network.

## Coverage problems

Report a dead spot to the facilities portal with the floor and the nearest desk number. A single user whose colleagues connect fine is a device problem and goes to the service desk instead. Home Wi-Fi is the user's own; the company does not support it beyond the VPN note.
