# Leaver Offboarding

## On the last day

The manager confirms the leave in the HR system before the end of the last working day. At 18:00 local time the account is disabled, active VPN sessions are dropped, and the authenticator enrolment is revoked. Mail to the address bounces, unless the manager has nominated a shared mailbox to receive it for the next 30 days.

## Equipment

The laptop, dock and badge are returned on the last day at an office, or collected by courier within five working days for a remote leaver. The drive is wiped in the build room and the wipe certificate is filed against the asset. A device not returned within fifteen working days is escalated to the manager and then to payroll.

## Data and access

The leaver's files on shared drives stay where they are. Their home drive is archived for 90 days and then deleted, unless a legal hold applies. Membership of every group, including the VPN group, is removed the same evening. A contractor whose end date arrives is treated as a leaver even if no one confirms it.

## Accounts that must survive

A mailbox under legal hold is disabled for sign-in but not deleted. Shared mailboxes the leaver owned transfer to the manager automatically.
